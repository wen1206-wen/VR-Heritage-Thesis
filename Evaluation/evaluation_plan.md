# Preliminary Evaluation Plan

The prototype will be evaluated in terms of:

1. Navigation usability
2. Ability to locate and understand deterioration hotspots
3. Understanding of heritage condition information
4. Perceived usefulness of VR during temporary site closure
5. VR comfort and usability

Potential methods include task observation, a short questionnaire, and a short interview.

Human participant evaluation will only begin after any required ethics approval has been obtained.
